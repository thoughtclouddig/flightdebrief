# CFI Discovery Sprint — 20 conversations

**Purpose: to falsify the continuity thesis, not to confirm it.** The council
could not tell us whether "nobody has said this out loud" means we are naming
an unnamed problem or building something nobody wants. Twenty calls can.

## Before you start

Copy `_template.md` to `01-firstname-lastname.md` (etc.) after each call.
Write it within the hour, while the wording is still exact. **Quotes are the
deliverable** — a summary loses the thing you needed.

Then run `./tally.sh` for the running score against the thresholds.

## Rules for the call

1. **Fifteen minutes. Not a demo.** Open with: *"I'm building something for
   flight training and I'm trying to find out if I've got the problem wrong.
   I'm not selling you anything, and I'd rather you tell me it's a bad idea."*
2. **Ask in order, then stop talking.** The failure mode is explaining the
   product mid-answer — the moment you do, they start answering the question
   you want instead of the one you asked.
3. **Never describe the feature before Q4.** Q4 is the only place the
   cross-instructor idea gets said out loud, and it only counts if they hear
   it cold.
4. **Write down the sentence, not the gist.**

## Who

Working CFIs, not school owners — the owner signs, but the CFI is the
adoption veto. A few chief instructors are worth including; they answer Q2
and Q5 differently. Sources: NAFI/SAFE membership (both have an established
norm of vendor concessions), the AOPA Flight Training Experience Award list,
your own airport, and warm intros.

## The decision rule (pre-registered — do not move it afterward)

| Signal | Threshold | Meaning if missed |
|---|---|---|
| **Q4 — cross-instructor finding changes what they do tomorrow** | **8 of 20** | The wedge is a demo trick, not a business. Keep the current homepage; do not ship stage two. |
| Q2 — can name a specific student and a specific cost from a handoff | 6 of 20 | The handoff pain is folklore, not felt. Stop selling "fewer repeat hours." |
| Q3 — shows you nothing written down AND is bothered by it | 8 of 20 | If they are untroubled, the narrative slot is empty because nobody wants it filled. |
| Q5 — spends >5 min/flight on write-up | 10 of 20 | "Minutes saved" is worth nothing; the whole CFI-adoption argument collapses. |

**Q1 has no threshold.** It exists to catch the endorsement-liability answer,
which would mean the continuity thesis is misdiagnosed regardless of how the
other four score.
