Phase 2 · Check-in 1LOCKED — user-authored

Concept: "Every Flight Builds"

The user wrote the concept directly, superseding the studio's shortlist. Core insight: the emotional center isn't "CFIs don't debrief well" — it's what happens to a student's brain after a demanding training flight. You land mentally saturated at the exact moment you're given the most valuable feedback of the lesson. The flight pushes you. The debrief makes sense of it. AfterFlight makes sure it stays with you.

Locked

Full timed script

0:00–0:08 The Flight — visceral cockpit shots, workload, quick handheld cuts.

0:08–0:16 Mental Saturation — landing, taxi, engine shutdown, sudden quiet on the student's face.

0:16–0:26 The Debrief — inside together, headset off, a genuinely good conversation.

0:26–0:33 The Problem — instructor's voice softens under the music; parking lot, already forgetting.

0:33–0:45 AfterFlight — enters at the midpoint, UI woven into the story, not a demo insert.

0:45–0:54 The Next Flight — morning light, phone glance, three focus items, purposeful energy.

0:54–1:00 Payoff — engine start, climb into morning light, logo, "Every flight builds on the one before it."

Concept spine — non-negotiable in every frame:
  1. The CFI reads as excellent and engaged, never rushed or negligent — that contrast is what makes the memory-saturation insight land.
  2. AfterFlight's product/UI does not appear before ~0:33 — the first half is pure recognition, no branding.
  3. No talking-head testimonials, no conventional screen-recording demo insert — UI beats are woven into the story's own camera language.
  4. One consistent student + one consistent CFI throughout — same two people, same wardrobe, same aircraft.
  5. Warm → cool → warm light arc: workload tension → quiet lamp-lit debrief → morning golden hour for the next flight and payoff.
Render segmentation for the 4×15s pipeline: A 0:00–0:15 (Flight → Saturation) · B 0:15–0:30 (Debrief → Problem starts) · C 0:30–0:45 (Problem finish → AfterFlight reveal) · D 0:45–0:60 (Next Flight → Payoff).

Phase 4LOCKED

Visual DNA

Six verbatim lines appended to every prompt from here on, alongside the concept spine.

1. Lighting: warm golden-hour and lamp-lit practical light, soft window bounce, warm-to-cool-to-warm arc across the film 2. Palette: navy and near-black ink, warm wood and skin mid-tones, a single desaturated orange accent reserved for the AfterFlight mark only 3. Capture: shot on a full-frame digital cinema camera, 35mm and 50mm prime lenses, slightly handheld documentary framing 4. Depth of field: shallow depth of field at f/1.8-f/2.8, soft falloff isolating faces and hands 5. Composition: intimate coverage — over-the-shoulder, medium close-up, rack focus; no static wide tripod shots 6. Authenticity: subtle 35mm film grain, gentle halation, natural skin texture, slight lens breathing, imperfect handheld micro-shake, no plastic AI-smooth skin
