# Visual DNA — "Every Flight Builds"

Six verbatim lines appended to the end of every image and video prompt in Phases 5–6,
alongside the concept spine (`concepts.md`).

1. **Lighting:** `warm golden-hour and lamp-lit practical light, soft window bounce, warm-to-cool-to-warm arc across the film`
2. **Palette:** `navy and near-black ink, warm wood and skin mid-tones, a single desaturated orange accent reserved for the AfterFlight mark only`
3. **Capture:** `shot on a full-frame digital cinema camera, 35mm and 50mm prime lenses, slightly handheld documentary framing`
4. **Depth of field:** `shallow depth of field at f/1.8-f/2.8, soft falloff isolating faces and hands from the background`
5. **Composition:** `intimate coverage — over-the-shoulder, medium close-up, rack focus between subject and instrument or screen; no static wide tripod shots`
6. **Authenticity spec:** `subtle 35mm film grain, gentle halation on highlights, natural skin texture and visible pores, slight lens breathing on focus pulls, imperfect handheld micro-shake, no plastic or airbrushed AI-smooth skin`
