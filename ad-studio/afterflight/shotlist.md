## Voiceover (per segment, for Phase 7 narration)
- **A:** "A training flight can ask everything you've got. You're learning. Adjusting. Making mistakes. Fixing them. Trying to stay ahead of the airplane."
- **B:** "Then you land, sit down with your instructor, and talk through what just happened. What worked. What didn't. What to work on next. It's some of the most valuable feedback you'll get."
- **C:** "And you're hearing it when you're tired, overloaded... and already starting to forget. AfterFlight turns that conversation into something you can keep. Your debrief. What went well. What needs work. And what comes next."
- **D:** "So before your next lesson, you don't have to remember where you left off. You already know what you're there to work on. Every flight builds on the one before it."
